FloatCam Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

FloatCam is a camera app that records you in a floating window while you use other apps. This policy explains what the app accesses and what leaves your device.

Your videos stay on your device

Everything you record is saved to your phone's own gallery (DCIM/Camera). FloatCam has no account system and no servers of its own. We never upload, view or store your videos, audio or images.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone — to record video and sound, only while you are recording.
  • Display over other apps — to show the floating camera window and recording controls.
  • Notifications — to show recording status and let you know when a video is saved.

Face detection happens on your device

The optional auto 9:16 framing uses Google ML Kit face detection to find your face position. The analysis runs entirely on your phone; camera frames are never sent anywhere.

Diagnostic data collected by Google ML Kit

The ML Kit library may send Google limited technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app package name and version, a per-installation identifier that does not identify you, performance metrics and error codes. It does not include your videos or images. The data is encrypted in transit and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

FloatCam is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

FloatCam 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日

FloatCam 是一款在你使用其他 App 时，用悬浮小窗录制自己的相机应用。本政策说明 App 会使用哪些权限，以及哪些数据会离开你的设备。

视频只保存在你的手机上

录制的视频保存在手机本地相册（DCIM/Camera）。FloatCam 没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器，我们不会上传、查看或保存你的视频、声音或图片。

权限说明

  • 相机和麦克风：仅在录制时用于拍摄画面和声音。
  • 悬浮窗：用于显示悬浮相机小窗和录制控制条。
  • 通知：显示录制状态，并在视频保存后提醒你。

人脸检测在本地完成

可选的「自动 9:16 构图」使用 Google ML Kit 在手机本地识别人脸位置，画面不会被发送到任何地方。

Google ML Kit 收集的诊断数据

ML Kit 组件可能向 Google 发送有限的技术数据，用于诊断和使用统计：设备信息（厂商、型号、系统版本）、App 包名和版本、不能识别你身份的安装标识、性能指标和错误代码。这些数据不包含你的视频或图片，传输时加密，并适用 Google 隐私政策。

儿童

FloatCam 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其个人信息。

政策更新

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面及生效日期。
